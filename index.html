HOW DATA MOVES THROUGH US

The pipeline, in order

This part genuinely is a sequence — each stage depends on the one before it.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Pull data in from source systems, files, or APIs, on a schedule or in real time.

  2. 02

    Transform

    Clean, standardise, and reshape it into something a report or a model can actually trust.

  3. 03

    Store

    Land it in a warehouse or database structured for how it will be queried later.

  4. 04

    Analyze

    Turn it into dashboards, reports, and metrics people check without being asked to.

  5. 05

    Decide

    The whole point — numbers someone can act on with confidence.
